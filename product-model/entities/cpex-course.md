# CPEx course

A course a student says, in the Course Planning Exercise, that they intend to take.

## Information kept

- **Course** — which course it is
- **Rank** — its place in the student's order of preference
- **Degree requirement** — what the course counts towards: Essential Major, Essential Second Major, Elective or Unrestricted Elective
