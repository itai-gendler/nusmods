# NUSMods website

The website at nusmods.com, usable on phones and installable on a home screen.
Everything is open without signing in: Today, Timetable, Optimiser, Courses,
Venues, Planner and Settings. Only the Course Planning Exercise asks students
to sign in with their NUS account.
